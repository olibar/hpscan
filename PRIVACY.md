# Scan to Me - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-06

Scan to Me (the iOS companion app of hpscan) does not collect, store or
transmit any personal data.

The app communicates only with HP printers on your local network in order to
receive scanned documents. Scanned files are saved on your device, in the
location you choose: the app's own folder (visible in the Files app), a
folder you pick in the Files app, or the Photos library. Nothing is sent to
us or to any third party. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and
no tracking, and it has no server component.

Permissions the app asks for and why:

- Local Network: to find your printer (Bonjour) and exchange scan data with it.
- Photos (add only, optional): to add JPEG scans to your Photos library when
  you enable that option in Settings.

The same applies to the hpscan desktop client in this repository: it talks
only to the printer and writes files to the folder you configure.
